The Third Ingredient Isn’t In Anybody’s Kitchen

Polyphenol content varies by an enormous margin depending on the flowers, the season, and how warm it got in the jar on the way to you.

Mistake two. The cinnamon.

Buying Ceylon was the right instinct. But ground cinnamon starts losing the compound that does the work from the day it’s ground, and the useful part barely dissolves. Stirring it into warm water is mostly just making the water brown.

To get a working amount out of a spice jar you’d be eating several heaped teaspoons a day. With the cheap species, that’s the liver problem. With the good species, most of it goes straight through you.
